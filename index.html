METHOD

Not a verdict. A visual model.

FacialTier converts visible geometry into normalized indicators. It does not determine attractiveness, personality, value, health or social status.

01 · LANDMARKS

Find structure

Facial landmarks locate key points around the eyes, nose, mouth, cheeks and jaw.

02 · NORMALIZE

Compare ratios

Distances are normalized against face dimensions so the model focuses on proportions rather than raw pixels.

03 · ESTIMATE

Map a tier

Several proxies are combined into a transparent illustrative estimate. A different photo can produce a different result.

MODEL NOTE

Context matters.

Camera distance, focal length, head tilt, shadows, expression and image quality can all move the visible landmarks. Treat your result as a repeatable snapshot, not a universal truth.
